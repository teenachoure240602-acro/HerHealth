KNOWLEDGE IS SELF-CARE

Understand your health. Make informed choices.

General information to help you ask informed questions and recognize when you may need professional medical advice.

Menstrual Health

Cycle timing and flow can vary. Tracking dates and changes may help you discuss patterns with a clinician.

  • Record period start dates if useful.
  • Note unusual changes in pain or bleeding.

Understanding Symptoms

Symptoms can have many causes. A log organizes observations but cannot identify a cause.

  • Record when symptoms began.
  • Describe severity and relevant notes.

Everyday Well-being

Supportive routines may include rest, balanced meals, movement suited to you, and social support.

When to Seek Help

Seek urgent care for sudden severe symptoms. Ask a qualified healthcare professional about persistent or worsening concerns.
